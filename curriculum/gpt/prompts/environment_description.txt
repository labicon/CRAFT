You are working with a quadruped robot capable of walking in a manner similar to a dog. 
The primary task in the environment is to navigate the robot toward a goal position while avoiding obstacles.
A low-level controller for the robot is already available; 
therefore, the learning objective is limited to generating high-level velocity commands in the form of [linear_velocity_x, linear_velocity_y, yaw_angular_velocity] that will lead to goal position.
The episode terminates once the robot successfully reaches the target position.

The size of the environment is 10*10 square.
The task is defind as success if all robots are within distance 1 from the target position.
The obstacle is a box with edge length of 1, and its center position is given.
If the distance between two robot is smaller than 0.25, it is defined as collision and the environment terminates.
