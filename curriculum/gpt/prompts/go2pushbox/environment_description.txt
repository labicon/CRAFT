You are working with a quadruped robot capable of walking in a manner similar to a dog.
The primary task in the environment is for two robots to coordinate and push a heavy box to a randomly assigned target position.
A low-level controller for the robot is already available;
therefore, the learning objective is limited to generating high-level velocity commands in the form of [linear_velocity_x, linear_velocity_y, yaw_angular_velocity].

**Task Success Criteria:**
The task is considered successful when the box's center is within 0.5 meters of the target position.
The target position is randomized each episode: x in [2.0, 3.5], y in [-1.5, 1.5].

**Objective & Coordination:**
1.  **Transport:** Push the box to the target position. The target can be in any direction relative to the box's starting position.
2.  **Alignment:** The two agents must coordinate their pushing forces to move the box efficiently toward the target while preventing the box from rotating excessively.
3.  **Technique:** Agents should push the box with their head links (`Head_upper`, `Head_lower`) as the primary contact.
4.  **Collision Constraint:** Contact between the box and non-head body parts (base, hips, thighs, calves, feet) should be minimized and penalized.

**Object Properties:**
-   **Box Dimensions:** 1.0m (length, x) * 1.0m (width, y) * 0.8m (height, z).
-   **Reference:** The box's position is defined by its geometric center.
-   **Spacing:** Agents must maintain sufficient spacing to push without colliding.
Agent 0 is colored with blue and Agent 1 is colored with green.
If the distance between two robot is smaller than 0.5, it is defined as collision.
