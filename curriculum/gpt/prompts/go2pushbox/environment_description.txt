You are working with a quadruped robot capable of walking in a manner similar to a dog. 
The primary task in the environment is for two robots to coordinate and push a heavy box forward (positive x-direction) as far as possible.
A low-level controller for the robot is already available; 
therefore, the learning objective is limited to generating high-level velocity commands in the form of [linear_velocity_x, linear_velocity_y, yaw_angular_velocity].

**Task Success Criteria:**
The task is considered successful when the x-coordinate of the box's center reaches or exceeds 3.5 meters (x >= 3.5).

**Objective & Coordination:**
1.  **Transport:** Push the box forward along the positive x-axis to the target distance of 3.5m.
2.  **Alignment:** Maintain the box's center at y=0 to ensure straight movement. The two agents must coordinate their pushing forces to prevent the box from rotating or drifting sideways.
3.  **Technique:** Agents must use their heads to apply force to the box. Using legs or other body parts for pushing is suboptimal and should be avoided.

**Object Properties:**
-   **Box Dimensions:** 0.5m (length, x) * 1.5m (width, y) * 0.5m (height, z).
-   **Reference:** The box's position is defined by its geometric center.
-   **Spacing:** The box's 1.5m width allows two agents to push side-by-side. Agents must maintain sufficient spacing to push in parallel without colliding.
Agent 0 is colored with blue and Agent 1 is colored with green.
If the distance between two robot is smaller than 0.5, it is defined as collision.
